How it works

Three steps from idea
to printed.

Every shop publishes its own price matrix, so the number you see is the number you pay.

01

Find a shop near you

Filter by service, material, rating, lead time and distance on the map.

02

Build the order, see the price

Size, material, finishing, quantity — the quote recalculates as you type.

03

Approve the proof, then collect

The shop sends a digital proof. Approve it, track production, scan to pick up.

Transparent pricing

The price matrix,
out in the open.

Every shop sets its own rates per square foot, per material, plus minimum charges and finishing fees. TarpaLink computes the total — including quantity discounts and rush surcharges — the moment you change a spec.

  • No hidden fees — the breakdown is itemised before checkout.
  • Quantity tiers apply automatically, not on request.
  • Compare the same job across shops in one table.
